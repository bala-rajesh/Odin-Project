Briyani Recpies

HOME

Description

  • Main Ingredients
  • 500 g chicken (bone-in or boneless)
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 large onions (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2–3 green chilies (slit)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ cup yogurt (curd)
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • 2–3 tbsp cooking oil or ghee
  • Whole Spices
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4–5 green cardamoms
  • 1 black cardamom (optional)
  • 4–5 cloves
  • 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 star anise (optional)
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • Ground Spices
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1–2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Rice Cooking
  • Water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 bay leaf
  • A few cloves and cardamoms (optional)
  • Optional Garnishes
  • Fried onions (birista)
  • Saffron soaked in warm milk or a pinch of food color
  • Cashews
  • Raisins
  • Lemon juice
  • Boiled eggs
  1. Chicken biryani begins with preparing the rice and marinating the chicken. Wash 2 cups of basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 30 minutes. In a bowl, combine 500 g of chicken with ½ cup yogurt, 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste, ½ teaspoon turmeric powder, 1½ teaspoons red chili powder, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, ½ teaspoon cumin powder, 1 teaspoon garam masala, salt to taste, and half of the chopped mint and coriander leaves. Mix well and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat 2–3 tablespoons of oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot and fry 2 thinly sliced onions until golden brown. Remove half of the fried onions for garnishing. In the same pot, add 2 bay leaves, 4 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, a 1-inch cinnamon stick, 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, and ½ teaspoon black peppercorns. Sauté the spices until fragrant, then add 2 chopped tomatoes and 2–3 slit green chilies. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  3. Add the marinated chicken to the pot and cook over medium heat for about 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally. Cook until the chicken is almost done and the masala becomes thick and aromatic. Taste the gravy and adjust the salt or spices if needed. This flavorful chicken mixture forms the base of the biryani.
  4. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of water to a boil with salt and a few whole spices if desired. Drain the soaked rice and cook it until it is about 70–80% done. The grains should still have a slight bite, as they will finish cooking during the steaming process. Drain the rice immediately to prevent overcooking.
  5. Spread the partially cooked rice evenly over the cooked chicken. Top it with the reserved fried onions, the remaining mint and coriander leaves, a drizzle of ghee, and saffron milk if using. Cover the pot tightly with a lid, or seal the edges with dough, and cook on very low heat for 20–25 minutes. Allow the biryani to rest for another 10 minutes after turning off the heat.
  6. the pot carefully and gently fluff the biryani with a fork or large spoon without breaking the rice. Serve the chicken biryani hot with onion raita, mirchi ka salan, a fresh onion and cucumber salad, lemon wedges, or boiled eggs. The combination of fragrant rice, tender chicken, and aromatic spices makes this a rich and satisfying meal.
